# Niche Report (Phase 0, v0.1)

Date: 2026-10-20. Evidence is directional: RPM tables online are mostly vendor/SEO compilations, not official YouTube data.

## Key findings
- **RPM** (US-weighted, directional): finance $10-25+, tech/AI $8-25, education/science $5-12 (AIR Media-Tech sample median for Education & Science: ~$10.22 vs ~$2.30 overall), history ~$5-10, gaming/kids lowest.
- **Policy (decisive).** Per reports of YouTube's July 2026 guidance, three categories are non-monetizable: generic/repetitive/template videos; content built to distress or manipulate; **AI personas giving health, finance or legal advice**. Reviews are channel-level. (Single-chain sourcing via TechCrunch/HackerNoon, so verify against YouTube's own policy page in Phase 1.)
- **API:** unverified projects created after 28 Jul 2020 are restricted to **private** uploads until an API compliance audit passes (official docs). Upload quota cost is disputed (1,600 units vs. a separate 100/day upload bucket), so we measure it in Cloud Console.

## Scoring (1-5, higher is better)

## Recommendation
**Evergreen science and technology explainers ("how X works", "why Y happens", history of inventions).**
- Lowest policy risk: no health/finance/legal advice, not news rehash.
- Evergreen: videos keep earning, so watch hours compound.
- Free-stack fit: facts from Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons (CC-licensed visuals), easy to fact-check, strong Shorts clip potential.
- Originality plan to avoid the "template" flag: multiple series formats, varied structure, unique analogies, custom diagrams, a consistent persona.

Fallback: psychology/literary analysis. Rejected: finance (policy), AI news (perishable, crowded).
